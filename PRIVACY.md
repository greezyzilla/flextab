# FlexTab — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 June 2026_

FlexTab is a browser extension that replaces your new tab page with a workspace
to save, organize, and sync your tabs across devices. This policy explains what
data FlexTab handles and why.

> **Replace `api.flextab.app` and the contact email below with your real values
> before publishing.** This is a starting draft, not legal advice.

## What we collect

FlexTab only collects what is needed to provide the product:

- **Account information** — when you sign in with Google or email, we receive
  your name, email address, and (for Google) your profile picture. This is used
  solely to authenticate you and sync your data.
- **Your saved tabs** — the URLs, page titles, and favicons of tabs you choose
  to save into spaces, projects, categories, and groups.
- **Open tab information** — to show your currently open tabs, FlexTab reads the
  titles and URLs of tabs in your browser using the `tabs` permission. This is
  read on-device and is **not** transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly save
  a tab.
- **Device information** — when signed in, we record a per-install identifier and
  your browser's User-Agent string so you can see which devices have used your
  account and when each last synced. We do not use it for tracking or advertising.

We do **not** collect browsing history, keystrokes, form data, or the content of
pages you visit. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Where your data is stored

- **Locally**, in your browser via `chrome.storage` (and synced across your own
  devices via Chrome's built-in storage sync).
- **On our backend** at `https://api.flextab.app`, if you are signed in, so your
  workspace is available across devices. Data is associated with your account.

## How we use your data

Your data is used only to operate FlexTab: authenticating you, storing your
saved tabs, and syncing them across your devices. We do **not** sell, rent, or
share your personal data with third parties, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## Third-party services

- **Google Sign-In** — used for authentication. Your use of Google Sign-In is
  also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete saved items
at any time from within the extension. To delete your account and all associated
data, contact us at the email below.

## Permissions we request

- `tabs` — to read open tab titles/URLs so you can view and save them.
- `storage`, `unlimitedStorage` — to store your workspace locally.
- `host_permissions` (the API host only) — to sync your data with the backend.

## Children's privacy

FlexTab is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.
